ABOUT THE BUSINESS

A local shop for everyday computer needs.

Elisha Internet Cafe is a family-run computer shop in Barangay Duljo Fatima, Cebu City. It has been operating for approximately eight years. The business started with 10 computers and has since grown to 25 computer units.

Its services include computer rental, internet access, gaming, printing, photocopying, scanning, typing, and encoding. The shop serves students, gamers, workers, and other people in the community.
