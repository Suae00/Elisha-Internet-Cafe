A LITTLE ABOUT US

Built around the
neighborhood.

Elisha Internet Cafe is a family-run computer shop located in Barangay Duljo Fatima, Cebu City. Operating for approximately eight years, the business has grown from 10 computers to 25 computer units. It provides computer rental, internet access, gaming, printing, photocopying, scanning, typing, and other convenient services for students and the local community.
